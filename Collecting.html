Start your own collection

Collecting vintage things is a fun hobby. You do not need a lot of money, just patience and a good eye. As the saying goes, one person's trash is another person's treasure.

A good first item is an old typewriter. You can read about how they work on the Wikipedia page about typewriters.

Every collector has a different reason. Some like the look of old things, some like the history, and some just enjoy the hunt for a rare find. A collection can start with just one item, such as a single postcard or a coin you found at a market.

Tips for new collectors

  • Pick one kind of item to start with.
  • Keep your things clean and dry.
  • Do not pay too much, ask for a better price.

Looking after your items

Old things need a little care. Keep paper, books and records away from sunlight and damp places, because light can fade colours and damp can cause mould. Dust metal items gently with a soft cloth. Never use strong cleaning products on something old, as they can damage the paint or the surface. If you are not sure how to clean an item, it is better to ask someone with experience first.

How much is it worth?

The price of a vintage item depends on its age, how rare it is and what condition it is in. An item that still works and has its original box is usually worth more. Before you buy, look at a few similar items to see what other people are paying. Remember that the story of an item matters too, and something with a story is often worth more to you than the price on the label.

Showing off your collection

Once you have a few items, show them off! A shelf, a glass cabinet or even a corner of a table can make a nice display. Group similar things together, for example all your cameras on one shelf, so they look neat and are easy to find.
